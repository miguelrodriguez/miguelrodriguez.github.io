Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

What we collect and how it’s used.

Types of Data Collected

Location Data

Our Application may collect information about Your device's location.

  • Purpose: We use Your location via GPS solely to verify Your position for the “Real Home Bonus” feature and to calculate flight distances within the simulator context.
  • Storage: This data is processed locally on Your device. We do not track Your movements when you are not using the Application, nor do We share your location history with third-party advertisers.

Usage Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when using the Service. Usage Data may include information such as Your Device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), browser type, browser version, the pages of our Service that You visit, the time and date of Your visit, the time spent on those pages, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

In-App Purchases and Subscription Data

The Application offers In-App Purchases and Subscriptions (FlightNexus PRO) processed via Apple's App Store.

  • Payment Information: We do not collect or store your credit card details. All financial transactions are handled securely by Apple Inc.
  • Subscription Status: We store the status of your subscription (e.g., Active/Expired) to unlock premium features locally and in Your private iCloud database.

Use of Your Personal Data

Purposes for using Personal Data.

We may use Personal Data for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain our Service, including to monitor the usage of our Service.
  • To manage Your Account: The Personal Data You provide can give You access to different functionalities of the Service that are available to You as a registered user.
  • To contact You: To contact You by email, telephone calls, SMS, or other equivalent forms of electronic communication, such as mobile application's push notifications regarding updates or informative communications related to the functionalities, products or contracted services, including the security updates, when necessary or reasonable for their implementation.

Data Storage (iCloud)

How FlightNexus stores your data.

FlightNexus uses Apple iCloud (CloudKit) to store your game data (Pilot Profile, Logbook, Flights).

  • Private Database: Your data is stored in your private iCloud container. We (the developers) do not have direct access to view or modify this data unless you explicitly choose to share it (e.g., for future multiplayer features).
  • Security: Your data is secured by Apple using the same industry-standard encryption used for all iCloud services.
