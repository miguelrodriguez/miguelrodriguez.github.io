What the app may store

Used to provide core functionality.

  • Pilot profile data you choose to enter (e.g., nickname)
  • Flight logs you create (e.g., routes, timestamps, notes)
  • App settings and preferences

Analytics & crash reports

Only if used/enabled in your version.

Depending on the version and configuration, FlightNexus may use crash reporting and/or basic analytics to improve stability and user experience. If enabled, third-party providers may collect technical information such as device type, OS version, and basic usage events.

Advertising

Only if a version includes ads.

If a version of FlightNexus includes advertising, ad providers may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads. You can manage ad tracking preferences in your device settings (where applicable).

Deletion & contact

How to remove your data.

You can delete locally stored data by removing the app from your device. If your version includes any syncing features, behavior may depend on the sync provider.
