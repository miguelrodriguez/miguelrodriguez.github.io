Summary

What this policy covers.

BARF Buddy may process certain information to provide and improve the service. By using the service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Types of data collected

Personal Data and Usage Data.

Personal Data

Depending on how you use the service, we may collect information such as Usage Data and other information you choose to send us (e.g., when contacting support).

Usage Data

Usage Data may be collected automatically and can include information such as device identifiers, diagnostic data, and how the service is accessed and used.

How data is used

Core reasons.

  • To provide and maintain the service
  • To improve stability and performance
  • To respond to support requests
  • To analyze usage trends and improve features

Sharing & disclosure

Limited situations.

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers (e.g., analytics or diagnostics) only as needed to operate or improve the service, comply with legal obligations, or protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

Children’s privacy

Under 13.

The service does not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under 13.
