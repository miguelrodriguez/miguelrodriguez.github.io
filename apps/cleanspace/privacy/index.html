Overview

How CleanSpace works in plain language.

CleanSpace is a photo library cleanup app. It scans your library to organize items into categories such as Duplicates, Screenshots, Similar Photos, and Large Videos, then lets you decide what to delete.

CleanSpace is designed to run fully offline for analysis and cleanup workflows.

Data Accessed

What the app reads from your photo library.

  • Photo library access permission (Full, Limited, or Denied)
  • Asset identifiers and media type (image/video)
  • Creation date, dimensions, duration, and screenshot flag
  • Estimated file sizes for reclaimable storage calculations
  • Generated on-device image feature prints for Similar Photos detection

CleanSpace does not require creating an account to use core cleanup functionality.

How Data Is Used

Why access is needed.

  • Scan and categorize assets (Duplicates, Screenshots, Similar Photos, Large Videos)
  • Build groups and suggested keep/delete recommendations
  • Show estimated reclaimable storage and cleanup results
  • Power review flows such as Swipe Clean and category detail screens
  • Execute deletion only after your explicit action

Processing and Storage

Where analysis happens and what is cached.

Photo analysis runs on your device. CleanSpace does not upload your photos or videos to our servers for scanning.

The app may store local app state (for example, cleanup session totals, cached analysis data, and purchase state) to improve performance and user experience.

Subscriptions and Purchases

How CleanSpace Pro billing works.

CleanSpace Pro is offered through auto-renewable subscriptions (monthly/yearly) via Apple's App Store.

  • Payments are processed by Apple, not by CleanSpace directly
  • CleanSpace uses StoreKit entitlements to unlock Pro features
  • Restore Purchases is available from within the app

Sharing and Disclosure

How information is shared.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share photo library content for advertising purposes.

Information may be disclosed only when required by law or valid legal process.

Your Controls

Permission and deletion controls.

  • You choose Photos permission level (Full, Limited, or Denied)
  • You can change permission in iOS Settings at any time
  • You decide what gets deleted; CleanSpace does not auto-delete without your action

Children's Privacy

Under 13.

CleanSpace is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided data inappropriately, contact us and we will address the request.
