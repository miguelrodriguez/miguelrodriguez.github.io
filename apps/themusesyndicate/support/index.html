What to include

These details help us understand the issue.

  • App version and macOS version
  • Mac model, chip, and memory, especially for processing or performance issues
  • Where it happens: Simple or Advanced creation, library, stems, voice tools, training, editor, mastering, music videos, or Premium
  • Steps to reproduce, what you expected, and what happened
  • The exact error message and whether essential model downloads are complete
  • A screenshot or diagnostic log, if you choose to share one

Share only the material needed to explain the issue. Remove private information from screenshots and logs, and do not send passwords, payment details, or recordings you do not want to share.

Common questions

Getting started and keeping your studio working.

Can I create music offline?

Yes. Complete installation and essential model downloads while connected to the internet. Generation and studio processing then run locally. Optional speech recognition languages may need an initial Apple download. Premium purchases, restoration, and renewal verification require internet access.

Generation or processing is taking a long time

Processing time depends on your Mac and the task. Check that model downloads have finished and that your Mac has free disk space. Include your Mac chip, memory, selected tool, and any error message when contacting support.

Where are my music and files?

Open Settings → Your Files → Show Files in Finder to locate your Muse folder. Keep a backup before removing files. Deleted audio and voices are not recreated automatically, and exports or backups saved elsewhere must be managed separately.

The app cannot access my studio folder

After current processing has finished, use Settings → Your Files → Renew Folder Access… to grant access again to the same Muse folder. Reopen the app afterward so its helpers can use the renewed permission.

Microphone recording is unavailable

Review The Muse Syndicate's microphone permission in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone. You can keep working with imported audio if you decline microphone access.

Which controls require Premium?

Premium adds manual duration and BPM, musical key and time signature, and generation in MP3 at 320 kbps or lossless FLAC. These controls are available in Simple and Advanced modes. The free version uses automatic musical parameters and MP3 at 128, 192, or 256 kbps; the other studio tools remain free.

How do I restore Premium?

Connect to the internet and use Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases with the App Store account that made the purchase. Your local studio profile is separate from your Apple Account. Verified access remains available offline through the valid subscription period.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Use Settings → Premium → Manage Subscription or open your App Store subscription settings. Removing local files, deleting a profile, resetting the studio, or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. For a refund request, visit Apple's Report a Problem service.

Can I use my own recordings, lyrics, and voices?

Use material you own or have permission to use, including training data and voices. Local processing does not replace those permissions. The Muse Syndicate does not claim ownership of your generated music or charge royalties on your output.
