Help with your studio.

Contact mrdevstudio@outlook.com. Include the app version, macOS version, Mac model, steps to reproduce and the message shown. Screenshots are optional. Do not send your Apple password, private owner license, payment details or audio you do not want to share.

Purchases and restoration

Use Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases with the App Store account that made the purchase. Your local studio profile is separate from your Apple Account. If pricing cannot load, reconnect to the internet; free local tools remain available.

Manage or cancel Premium

Use Settings → Premium → Manage Subscription or Apple subscription settings. Deleting a profile, clearing files, uninstalling or resetting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refund requests are handled by Apple at Report a Problem.

Your files

Use Settings → Your Files → Show Files in Finder. The app creates its studio storage automatically. Back up your studio files before removing anything. Deleting a file in Finder does not restore it automatically. Missing library records can be removed without recreating their audio. Referenced songs must first be removed from the library before becoming eligible for unused-file cleanup.

Folder permissions

If you chose an external studio folder in an earlier version, use Renew Folder Access in Settings if macOS stops allowing access to that folder. New installations use automatic app storage and do not ask you to choose a folder. Imported recordings remain usable without microphone permission. Change microphone permission in macOS System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Offline operation

Music and voice models are included with the app; no separate model download is required. Generation and studio processing run locally; internet outages do not disable local services. Purchases, restoring and confirming renewals need internet. Optional speech languages may need an initial Apple download.

Voice training and ownership

Use voices, recordings and training material you have permission to use. Keep backups of your training datasets and exported models.
