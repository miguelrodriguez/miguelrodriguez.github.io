Your music. Your Mac. Your control.

A local workflow that keeps the creative decisions with you.

Music generation and studio processing run locally on your Mac. Your audio and lyrics are not uploaded to a hosted generation service.

The Muse Syndicate does not claim ownership of the music you create or charge royalties on your output. You decide what to keep, edit, export, and share, whether you use the free version or Premium.

Local processing keeps your workflow private; it does not replace the rights and permissions needed for imported music, lyrics, or voices.

Keep creating offline

No online generation credits to buy and no hosted processing queue.

Once installation and essential model downloads are complete, your local studio works without an internet connection. Processing time depends on your Mac and the work you ask it to do.

Purchase and restoration require an internet connection. Verified Premium access remains available offline through its valid subscription period.

The studio tools stay free. Premium adds musical and output controls.
