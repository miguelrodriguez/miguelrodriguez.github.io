Privacy Policy

The Muse Syndicate · Updated October 9, 2026 · Developer: Miguel Rodriguez, Dominican Republic.

Local processing and storage

Music generation, voice processing, model training, audio editing, video rendering and supported on-device transcription run on your Mac. The app does not upload your audio, lyrics, trained voices or local profile images to a remote processing service. Profiles, library records and your creations are stored locally. The app creates its studio storage automatically; an existing studio folder chosen in an earlier version is preserved. The app does not provide cloud profile synchronization.

Apple services

Music and voice models are included with the app and updated when you install a new app version. Apple may download speech recognition language assets. Premium purchases, restoration and subscription management use Apple's StoreKit and App Store services. Apple processes account, purchase and payment information under its own policies. The app checks verified purchase information such as product identifier, expiry and revocation to determine access. The app does not receive your Apple Account password, payment card details or Apple Account email address.

Owner licenses

A signed owner license can grant permanent access independently of subscriptions. It is verified locally and stored in the macOS keychain, without an online license server. Resetting studio files does not cancel Apple subscriptions or remove owner access.

Microphone and imported files

The app records from your microphone only with macOS permission. You can decline and continue with imported audio. If you open a remote media URL, the requested provider receives your connection and request; this does not turn local audio processing into remote processing.

Tracking and diagnostics

The app contains no advertising trackers and does not send usage analytics to the developer. Local diagnostic logs stay on your Mac unless you choose to share them. Apple may collect App Store, purchase, crash or platform diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its policies.

Deletion and your control

Use Settings to manage profiles, unused-file quarantine, voices and training files. Profile deletion removes its personal records and files while preserving shared studio resources and files referenced by other profiles. A complete reset removes local profiles, studio data and preferences; installed base models and owner access are retained. Deletion is permanent where the app explicitly says so. Keep backups of material you need. A local reset does not cancel a subscription: manage it through Apple.

Support and website

If you email support, the developer receives the information and attachments you choose to send and uses them to respond and diagnose the reported issue. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub and your email provider process connection or correspondence data under their respective policies. Do not send passwords or sensitive recordings unnecessarily.

Changes and contact

This page will be updated when app behavior changes. For privacy questions or support-correspondence deletion requests, contact mrdevstudio@outlook.com.
