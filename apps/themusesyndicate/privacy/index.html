Local processing and studio data

Your creative work is processed and stored locally.

Music generation, voice processing, custom voice and musical style training, audio editing, mastering, and music video rendering run on your Mac. The app does not upload your audio, lyrics, or trained voices to a hosted generation or remote processing service.

The local data you choose to create or import can include prompts, lyrics, songs, playlists, reference audio, microphone recordings, stems, training datasets, custom voices and styles, project files, and exports. Your creations and library are saved in the Muse folder you choose. Profiles, preferences, temporary files, and library backups are also stored locally.

The app does not provide cloud profile synchronization. If you place files in a folder backed up or synchronized by another service, that service handles them according to your settings and its own policy.

Microphone and file permissions

You choose which recordings and files to bring into the studio.

The app uses your microphone for recording only after you grant macOS permission. You can decline or revoke microphone access in macOS System Settings and continue working with imported audio.

You choose the files and studio folder the app can access. Access is used to import, organize, process, save, and export your creative work.

Downloads and optional connections

Internet access supports setup and services you choose to use.

Apple provides model downloads and updates. Speech recognition may download an Apple language model before its first use; supported on-device transcription is processed on your Mac.

Once installation and essential model downloads are complete, local generation and studio processing work offline. Downloads require a connection to the service delivering them.

If you open media from a remote URL, the provider you choose receives the connection and request, which can include your IP address. Opening a remote file does not upload your local studio content for hosted processing.

Premium and Apple services

Apple handles subscription purchases and payment information.

Premium uses Apple's StoreKit and App Store services for purchases and restoration. The app checks verified purchase status, including the product identifier, subscription validity, and expiration or revocation information, to determine access. The app does not receive your Apple Account password, payment card details, or Apple Account email address.

Purchase, restoration, and renewal verification require internet access. Verified Premium access remains available offline through its valid subscription period. Apple processes account, transaction, and payment information under its App Store privacy policy.

If you use a signed owner license, it is verified locally and stored in the macOS keychain without an online license server. Deleting profiles or resetting studio files does not cancel an Apple subscription or remove owner access.

Tracking and diagnostics

No advertising trackers or developer usage analytics in the app.

The Muse Syndicate does not include advertising trackers or send usage analytics to the developer. Diagnostic logs remain on your Mac unless you choose to share them.

Apple may process App Store, purchase, crash, or platform diagnostics according to your Apple settings and its policies. This is separate from the app's local creative processing.

Information you share with support

Support receives only what you choose to send.

If you email mrdevstudio@outlook.com, we receive your email address, message, and any details or attachments you provide, such as app and macOS versions, screenshots, recordings, or diagnostic logs.

We use this information to respond, diagnose the reported issue, and handle your request. Do not send passwords, payment details, or sensitive recordings unnecessarily. Support email is handled through our email provider, which processes correspondence under its own privacy policy.

We retain support correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant support records, and meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of information you sent directly to us.

Your control, retention, and deletion

Manage your local files, backups, and exports.

You decide what to import, record, generate, train, keep, export, and share. Local studio data remains on your Mac until you delete it. Use the app's file and profile controls, or Settings → Your Files → Show Files in Finder, to review your stored material.

Keep backups before deleting files or resetting the studio. Deleted audio and voices are not recreated automatically. Profile deletion can preserve shared resources and files referenced by other profiles, and a studio reset can retain installed base models. Exports and backups saved elsewhere must be removed separately.

We cannot remotely retrieve or delete files that exist only on your Mac. Deleting local profiles or files, resetting the studio, or uninstalling the app does not cancel Premium; manage the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Exports, sharing, and third parties

Your chosen destinations control exported content.

Exports are created at your request. When you share audio, lyrics, voices, videos, or other files through another app or service, that destination handles the content according to its own terms and privacy practices.

We do not sell your support information or use your local creative content for advertising. Information you provide directly to us may be disclosed to service providers needed to handle support, or when required by law. This does not give us access to content stored only on your Mac.

The Muse Syndicate does not claim ownership of the music you create or charge royalties on your output. You remain responsible for the rights and permissions needed for imported music, lyrics, voices, and training material.

This website and external links

Hosting and linked services have their own privacy practices.

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information when you visit, according to the GitHub Privacy Statement. The pages do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts.

Links to Apple, GitHub, and other services take you to destinations operated by those providers. Review their policies when you use them.
