Interpretation and Definitions

Key terms used in this policy.

Interpretation

Words with the initial letter capitalized have meanings defined under the following conditions. These definitions have the same meaning whether they appear in singular or plural.

Definitions

  • Application: ArchNexus, the software program provided by the Company.
  • Company (“We”, “Us”, “Our”): Miguel Rodriguez.
  • Country: Dominican Republic.
  • Device: any device that can access the Service, such as a computer, cellphone, or tablet.
  • Personal Data: information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual.
  • Project Data: the workspace information you choose to create in the app, including projects, budgets, catalogs, pricing, and attached reference files.
  • Service: the Application.
  • Usage Data: data collected automatically, generated by use of the Service or its infrastructure.

Collecting and Using Your Personal Data

What we collect and how it is used.

Types of Data Collected

Project Data

ArchNexus is designed to handle professional workspace information that you choose to create or import. Depending on how you use the Service, this may include:

  • Project names, sections, subsections, and other organizational structure
  • Budgets, line items, quantities, units, taxes, waste factors, and pricing values
  • Catalog data for materials, interiors, labor, services, permits, logistics, contingency, and custom costs
  • Client and vendor information that you choose to enter
  • Files and attachments such as plans, images, renders, and documents
  • Budget snapshots, dashboard data, and CSV export content generated from your workspace

Usage Data

Usage Data may be collected automatically when using the Service. It may include information such as your device type, operating system version, identifiers, and diagnostic data used to operate, maintain, and improve the Service.

Support Data

If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, app version, device details, screenshots, exported files, and a description of the issue.

Use of Your Personal Data

Purposes for using information related to the Service.

We may use Personal Data and Project Data for purposes such as:

  • To provide and maintain the Service
  • To store, organize, calculate, and display your project and budget information
  • To support CSV exports and other outputs you choose to generate
  • To synchronize your data across devices when iCloud sync is enabled
  • To respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues
  • To monitor usage, improve the Service, and protect the reliability and security of the app

Data Storage (iCloud)

How sync works when enabled.

ArchNexus is described as supporting iCloud sync across Apple devices. If iCloud sync is enabled in your version, project data and related workspace content may be stored in or transmitted through Apple's iCloud services so that the same information can be available on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

  • Private storage: Data synchronized through iCloud is generally stored within your Apple account environment.
  • Apple services: Apple provides the iCloud infrastructure and applies its own privacy and security controls to that service.
  • Your choice: If your version of the app allows it, you control whether to use iCloud sync.

CSV Exports

Files generated from your workspace.

ArchNexus may allow you to export CSV files generated from your project data. These exports may contain cost, pricing, catalog, or other workspace information based on the mode you choose, such as Full or Costs-Only.

Exported files are created at your request. Once exported, saved, or shared, they are handled according to the apps, destinations, and system features you choose to use on your device.

Under certain circumstances, we may be required to disclose your information if required by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities.

Other Legal Requirements

We may disclose information in good faith when necessary to:

  • Comply with a legal obligation
  • Protect and defend the rights or property of the Company
  • Prevent or investigate possible wrongdoing related to the Service
  • Protect the personal safety of users or the public
  • Protect against legal liability

Retention of Your Personal Data

How long information may be kept.

We retain Personal Data only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, and as required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Project Data that you create in the app may remain stored on your device or within your enabled sync services until you delete it or remove the app, subject to the behavior of your device and Apple services.

Delete Your Personal Data

Your controls over workspace information.

You choose what information to add to the Service, what files to attach, whether to export CSV data, and whether to use iCloud sync if your version supports it.

You may be able to update or delete information directly within the app. You can also contact us if you have questions about information you provided directly to us through support channels.
