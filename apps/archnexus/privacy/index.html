Overview

How ArchNexus is intended to handle professional project data.

ArchNexus is a professional workspace for architects, interior designers, and construction professionals. It is designed to help organize projects, budgets, materials, pricing, and client-ready PDF reports.

The app may store and process the information you choose to add to your workspace, including project structure, budget data, catalog entries, and attached files. If iCloud sync is enabled, project data may also sync through your Apple iCloud account.

Project Data You Create

Information you choose to enter into the app.

  • Project names, section and subsection structure, and workspace organization
  • Budget lines, material costs, sales pricing, and multi-currency values
  • Catalog information for materials and interior items
  • Notes, quantities, pricing assumptions, charts, analytics, and report content

Files and Attachments

Plans, images, renders, and documents you choose to add.

ArchNexus may allow you to attach plans, images, renders, and documents to projects. These files are added by your choice as part of your project workspace.

  • Attached files are used to support project organization and reporting.
  • Generated PDF reports may include information and branding from your workspace.
  • Exported reports and files are stored or shared according to the actions you take on your device.

iCloud Sync

Sync across Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

ArchNexus is described as supporting iCloud sync across Apple devices. If sync is enabled in your version, project data and related workspace content may be stored or transmitted through Apple's iCloud services so it can stay available on your Mac, iPhone, and iPad.

Apple provides the iCloud infrastructure and applies its own privacy and security controls to that service.

PDF Reports and Exports

Client-ready documents generated from your workspace.

ArchNexus may generate polished PDF reports using your project data, pricing, catalogs, attachments, and branding.

  • Report content depends on the information you add to the app.
  • Exported files may be saved, previewed, printed, or shared using Apple system features available on your device.

Support Requests

Information you choose to send us.

If you contact support by email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address, screenshots, exported PDFs, device details, sync state, and issue description.

We use support information only to respond to your request, troubleshoot problems, and improve the product.

Sharing and Disclosure

How information may be shared.

We do not publish your private project data publicly unless you choose to export or share it.

Information may be disclosed when required by law, valid legal process, or when needed to respond to your direct support request.

Your Controls

How you control project information.

  • You choose what projects, budgets, files, and branding assets to add.
  • You choose whether to export, print, or share generated reports.
  • You choose whether to use iCloud sync if your version supports it.

Children's Privacy

Under 13.

ArchNexus is intended for professional use and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes to this Policy

Updates over time.

We may update this Privacy Policy over time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
